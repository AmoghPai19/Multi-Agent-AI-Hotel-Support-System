# Booking & Reservation Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-BOOK-001 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 2.1
**Owner:** Revenue Management & Front Office | **Review Cycle:** Annual

## 1. Purpose
This policy governs how reservations are created, held, confirmed, and modified across all direct channels (property website, call center, front desk) and third-party channels (OTAs, GDS, travel agents).

## 2. Reservation Confirmation
- A reservation is considered **confirmed** only when the guest receives a confirmation number and, where a guarantee is required, a valid payment method has been successfully authorized.
- Confirmation emails must include: confirmation number, room type, rate plan, check-in/check-out dates, total estimated charges (room + tax + mandatory fees), and the applicable cancellation policy verbatim.
- Verbal holds made by phone or chat are non-binding and expire after 24 hours unless guaranteed with a card.

## 3. Rate Plans & Guarantee Types
| Rate Type | Guarantee Required | Modifiable | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flexible / Best Available Rate (BAR) | Card on file, not charged at booking | Yes, free up to cutoff | Standard default rate |
| Advance Purchase / Non-Refundable | Full charge at time of booking | No | Discounted 10-20% vs. BAR |
| Member / Loyalty Rate | Card on file | Yes | Requires active loyalty account |
| Corporate / Negotiated | Per contract terms | Per contract | Requires valid corporate ID or code |

## 4. Minimum Age to Book
The primary guest booking or checking in must be **18 years or older** (21 in select jurisdictions/properties — confirm local addendum) and present valid government-issued photo ID matching the reservation name at check-in.

## 5. Rate Guarantee & Price Matching
- Best Rate Guarantee claims must be submitted within 24 hours of booking via the official website channel, comparing an identical, publicly bookable rate (same dates, room type, cancellation terms) on a different site.
- Valid claims are honored by matching the lower rate and applying an additional discount as defined in the current promotional terms (subject to change; current terms supersede this summary).

## 6. Overbooking Buffer
Properties may accept reservations up to an internally approved overbooking percentage (typically 3-8% above physical inventory) based on historical no-show and cancellation forecasting. See **POL-OVBK-008 (Overbooking & Walked Guest Policy)** for guest remedies if this results in an oversold night.

## 7. Third-Party (OTA) Bookings
- OTA-booked reservations are bound by the rate and cancellation terms displayed on the OTA at time of booking; the property cannot unilaterally impose stricter terms at check-in.
- Payment processing for OTA "merchant model" bookings is handled by the OTA; the property must not double-charge the guest's card.
- Any modification requested at the property for an OTA booking should be routed back through the OTA where the original terms require it, unless the OTA has delegated modification authority to the property.

## 8. Data Required at Booking
Full name, valid contact email, phone number, arrival/departure dates, and a valid payment method (unless a Pay-at-Hotel rate applies, in which case a card may still be required for incidental hold). See **POL-PRIV-013 (Privacy & Data Protection Policy)** for handling of this data.

## 9. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- Never confirm a reservation without a successful payment authorization or explicit "pay at hotel" rate eligibility.
- Never quote a rate that has not been validated against current inventory/rate availability in the same request cycle.
- Any reservation involving a name mismatch, third-party payment card, or suspicious multi-room/same-day booking pattern must be flagged for human review per fraud-prevention guidelines (see POL-COND-009 §5).
