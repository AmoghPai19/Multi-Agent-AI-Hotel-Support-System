
# Refund Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-REFUND-003 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 2.0
**Owner:** Finance / Guest Services | **Review Cycle:** Annual

## 1. Purpose
Governs when refunds are owed, how they are calculated, and processing timelines. Works in conjunction with POL-CXL-002 (Cancellation) and POL-DISP-020 (Booking Disputes & Chargebacks).

## 2. Refund Eligibility Matrix
| Scenario | Eligible for Refund | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Cancelled within free cancellation window | Yes | 100% |
| Cancelled after cutoff, refundable rate | Partial | Total minus penalty (usually 1 night + tax) |
| Non-refundable rate, cancelled any time | No (absent approved exception) | 0%, unless goodwill exception approved |
| Property-caused issue (overbooking, major maintenance failure, uninhabitable room) | Yes | 100% of affected night(s), possible additional compensation per POL-OVBK-008 |
| Duplicate/erroneous charge confirmed | Yes | Full amount of the erroneous charge |
| Guest dissatisfaction (service issue, not safety/health) | Case-by-case, service-recovery discretion | Partial credit or comp, per service recovery guidelines |
| Fraudulent booking / stolen card confirmed | Yes, per chargeback investigation | Full amount, handled through payment processor |

## 3. Processing Timelines
- Approved refunds are issued to the **original form of payment** within **5-10 business days**; the guest's bank may take an additional 3-5 business days to post the credit.
- Cash payments are refunded in cash at the property or via check if the guest has departed, within 10 business days.
- The system must never promise an exact same-day refund posting, since this depends on the card issuer.

## 4. Partial Refunds & Service Recovery Credits
- Service recovery credits (e.g., for a noise complaint, late housekeeping, minor amenity failure) are capped per the property's service recovery matrix (typically up to one night's room rate or a defined dollar cap, whichever is greater, requiring manager approval above that threshold).
- Any credit or refund exceeding the automated-agent approval threshold (default: **$150 USD equivalent**) requires human manager sign-off before issuance.

## 5. Denied Refund Requests
When a refund is denied, the guest must be given:
1. The specific policy clause the denial is based on.
2. The original terms shown to the guest at time of booking (rate plan and cancellation terms).
3. A clear path to escalate (manager review, or corporate guest relations) if they disagree.

## 6. Refunds Involving Third-Party Bookings (OTAs)
For bookings made through an OTA on the merchant model, the refund must be processed through the OTA's system, not directly by the property, to avoid duplicate refunds or ledger mismatches. The property may issue a compensatory credit directly for service issues, but the underlying room charge refund flows through the original payment channel.

## 7. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- Never issue a refund without verifying the original charge exists and matches the reservation ID.
- Never exceed the automated approval threshold without escalation.
- Always log: reservation ID, refund amount, reason code, approver (system or human), and timestamp for audit purposes.
- If the guest disputes the refund amount or timeline, direct them to the formal Complaint & Dispute Resolution process (POL-CDR-016).
