# No-Show Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-NOSHOW-004 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 1.4
**Owner:** Front Office / Revenue Management

## 1. Definition
A **no-show** is a confirmed reservation where the guest does not arrive by end of business day (typically the property's night audit cutoff, commonly 3:00-4:00 AM) on the scheduled arrival date, without prior cancellation or modification.

## 2. Standard No-Show Charge
- The full first night's room rate plus applicable taxes and mandatory fees is charged to the guaranteeing payment method.
- For non-refundable/prepaid rates, the full prepaid amount is already collected; no additional charge is applied, and no refund is issued.
- Remaining nights of a multi-night reservation are automatically released from inventory and cancelled without further penalty (unless the rate terms specify otherwise, e.g., non-refundable full-stay packages).

## 3. Late Arrivals vs. No-Shows
- Guests arriving after standard check-in but before the night-audit cutoff are **not** no-shows and must be honored at the originally booked rate, provided the reservation was guaranteed.
- If a guest calls ahead to notify of a late arrival (e.g., delayed flight), this must be noted on the reservation and the room held without additional guarantee requirements, up to the property's late-arrival guarantee limit (typically held until 11:59 PM or next-day audit).

## 4. Group & Block No-Shows
Individual no-shows within a group block are billed per the master account or individual folio as specified in the group contract (master bill vs. individual pay).

## 5. Disputing a No-Show Charge
Guests may dispute a no-show charge if they can demonstrate:
- They cancelled before the cutoff (system log/confirmation number required).
- The property had no room available upon a genuine, timely arrival attempt (walked-guest scenario — see POL-OVBK-008).
- A documented emergency prevented arrival (handled per POL-CXL-002 §4 exception process).

## 6. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- Do not apply a no-show charge before the property's local night-audit cutoff has passed.
- Always check for a late-arrival note on the reservation before flagging as a no-show.
- No-show disputes involving a claimed prior cancellation must be verified against the cancellation log before any reversal is issued; do not take the guest's verbal claim alone as sufficient to reverse a charge.
