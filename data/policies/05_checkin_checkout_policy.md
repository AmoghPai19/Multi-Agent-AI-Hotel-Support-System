# Check-In / Check-Out Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-CICO-005 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 1.6
**Owner:** Front Office

## 1. Standard Times
- **Check-in:** 3:00 PM property local time.
- **Check-out:** 11:00 AM property local time.
- Rooms are guaranteed available by check-in time for confirmed reservations; if unavailable, see §4 (Delayed Room Readiness).

## 2. Early Check-In / Late Check-Out
- Early check-in and late check-out are offered **subject to availability** and may incur a fee (commonly a percentage of the nightly rate, or a flat fee, escalating the closer to a full extra night).
- Loyalty program elite tiers may receive complimentary early/late check-in per program terms (see POL-LOY-015).
- Requests should be logged at booking or via pre-arrival communication where possible, though same-day requests are accommodated on a best-effort basis.

## 3. Identification & Guarantee at Check-In
- A valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID matching the reservation name is required.
- A credit/debit card or cash deposit is required to cover incidentals (see POL-SEC-019, Security Deposit Policy), even if the room itself was prepaid.
- Third-party check-ins (someone other than the named guest picking up a room) require the reservation holder's advance written authorization plus the picker-upper's own ID.

## 4. Delayed Room Readiness
If a confirmed, guaranteed reservation's room is not ready by check-in time:
1. Offer a complimentary amenity (e.g., beverage/lounge access) while the guest waits.
2. Provide a realistic time estimate and proactive updates.
3. If the delay exceeds 60 minutes past check-in time, offer a room upgrade if available, or a partial rate reduction per service recovery guidelines.
4. If no room becomes available at all that night, this escalates to the Overbooking & Walked Guest Policy (POL-OVBK-008).

## 5. Express / Late Check-Out Without Approval
Guests remaining in the room past check-out without an approved late check-out are subject to:
- A grace period (typically 30 minutes) with no charge.
- Beyond the grace period, a late check-out fee or a full additional night charge if past a defined threshold (commonly 6:00 PM), at management discretion, disclosed in advance where feasible.

## 6. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- Never confirm early check-in/late check-out as guaranteed; always phrase as "subject to availability."
- Always verify ID-name match logic conceptually maps to the reservation before an agent tells a guest a keycard/room will be issued.
- Escalate to a human agent any request for check-in by someone who is not the named guest and lacks documented authorization.
