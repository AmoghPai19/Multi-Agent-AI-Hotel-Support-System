# Group & Event Booking Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-GRP-007 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 1.3
**Owner:** Sales & Events

## 1. Scope
Applies to bookings of 10 or more rooms on peak-night(s), or any function-space/event booking (meetings, weddings, conferences), which are governed by a signed **Group Sales Contract**, not individual reservation terms.

## 2. Contract Requirements
Every group contract must specify: room block size and dates, attrition percentage and calculation method, cutoff date for the block to release, deposit schedule, cancellation/attrition penalties, master account vs. individual billing, and function space/catering minimums if applicable.

## 3. Attrition
- Attrition is the percentage of contracted rooms the group must pick up (typically 80-90%); if actual pickup falls short by the cutoff date, the group is liable for liquidated damages calculated on the shortfall at the contracted rate.
- Attrition liability is distinct from individual cancellation penalties and is billed to the group's master account, not individual guests.

## 4. Rooming Lists & Individual Reservations Within a Block
- Individual reservations tied to a group block follow the block's specific rate and cancellation terms, which may differ from standard published policy (e.g., a longer cancellation window like 30 days).
- Changes to the rooming list (adding/removing individual names) must be submitted by the group's designated contact/planner, not by individual attendees directly, unless the contract states otherwise.

## 5. Deposits
Standard staged deposit schedule: initial deposit at signing (commonly 25%), a second deposit at a mid-point milestone, and the balance due prior to arrival — exact percentages and dates are set per contract.

## 6. Force Majeure for Groups
Group contracts include a force majeure clause allowing penalty-free postponement or cancellation for qualifying events (natural disaster, government travel restriction, etc.) — see POL-FM-017 for the general force majeure definition used across all policies.

## 7. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- Automated agents must not modify group block terms, attrition calculations, or master billing arrangements — these require sales/events team and contract review.
- Individual attendee questions about a group rate's cancellation terms should be answered using the specific block's contracted terms if available in the reservation record, not the standard individual cancellation policy.
- Escalate any group-related billing or attrition dispute to the Sales/Events team.
