# Overbooking & Walked-Guest Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-OVBK-008 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 1.5
**Owner:** Revenue Management / General Manager

## 1. Purpose
Defines the mandatory remedies owed to a guest with a confirmed, guaranteed reservation who cannot be accommodated ("walked") due to the hotel being oversold. This is a guest-protection policy and a common source of legitimate compensation claims — the Compliance Agent must recognize it as a valid, non-discretionary obligation, not a goodwill gesture.

## 2. When This Policy Applies
A guest qualifies for walked-guest remedies when **all** of the following are true:
- The guest held a confirmed, guaranteed reservation for that specific night.
- The guest arrived by the property's stated check-in cutoff (or within any late-arrival grace period noted on the reservation).
- No room in the reserved category (or better) was available at the property.

## 3. Mandatory Remedies (Minimum Standard)
1. **Comparable or better accommodation** at a nearby property, arranged and paid for by the original hotel, including the rate difference if the alternate property is more expensive.
2. **Free transportation** to and from the alternate property.
3. **One free phone call / message** to notify family or business contacts of the changed location, or equivalent digital notice.
4. **Return transportation** back to the original property for the remainder of the stay, if applicable, at no cost.
5. A **make-good compensation** (loyalty points, a free night, or a cash-equivalent credit per current program terms) in addition to the above, as an apology gesture — amount defined in the current compensation matrix, not this document.
6. The original room rate is honored for the walked night at minimum; the guest must never end up paying more for the substitute night due to the property's error.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
Every walked-guest incident must be logged with: reservation ID, date/time of the walk decision, approving manager, alternate property and rate, transportation arranged, and compensation issued. This log is required for brand-standard audits and legal/regulatory exposure tracking.

## 5. Loyalty Elite Members
Members of the loyalty program's top tiers are generally protected from being walked wherever operationally possible; if unavoidable, they typically receive an enhanced compensation tier per program terms.

## 6. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- An automated agent must **never** deny that overbooking occurred if the reservation was confirmed and guaranteed and no room was available — this is a policy-guaranteed remedy, not negotiable down to nothing.
- Automated agents should escalate all walked-guest situations to an on-duty manager for real-time execution (arranging the alternate property) but must clearly communicate the guest's guaranteed minimum remedies while doing so, using the language in §3.
- Do not promise a specific dollar compensation amount beyond what is defined in the current compensation matrix; refer exact figures to the manager.
