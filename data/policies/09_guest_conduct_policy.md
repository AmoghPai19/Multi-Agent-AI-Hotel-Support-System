# Guest Conduct & Acceptable Use Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-COND-009 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 1.4
**Owner:** Security / Guest Relations

## 1. Purpose
Sets behavioral standards for guests and visitors, and the escalation ladder for violations, to keep staff and other guests safe while treating guests fairly and consistently.

## 2. Prohibited Conduct
- Violence, threats, or harassment (verbal, physical, or sexual) directed at staff, other guests, or visitors.
- Illegal activity on premises, including drug use/distribution beyond what is legally permitted, and underage drinking.
- Smoking in non-smoking rooms/areas (see POL-SMOKE-018).
- Exceeding posted maximum room occupancy.
- Unauthorized commercial activity (e.g., using a guest room as a business storefront) or subletting a room to a non-registered occupant.
- Property damage beyond normal wear and tear (see POL-DMG-010).
- Disturbing other guests (excessive noise, particularly between posted quiet hours, typically 10:00 PM-8:00 AM).

## 3. Escalation Ladder
1. **Verbal notice** from staff describing the specific issue and the expected correction.
2. **Written/formal warning** (in person or via written notice to the room) if the issue continues within the same stay.
3. **Eviction** without refund for the remaining nights, for repeated violations after warning, or immediately for severe safety violations (violence, weapons, illegal drug distribution), consistent with applicable innkeeper/lodging law in the property's jurisdiction.
4. **Law enforcement involvement** for any criminal conduct, at staff's discretion, regardless of prior warning steps.

## 4. Guest Rights During Escalation
- Guests must be given a clear, specific reason for any warning or eviction, in a manner consistent with local hospitality/innkeeper laws (many jurisdictions require reasonable notice and prohibit eviction "into the elements" without safe alternative arrangements, except in cases of immediate danger).
- Eviction decisions are not made by automated systems — they require an on-site manager or security lead's authorization.

## 5. Fraud & Suspicious Activity Indicators (for Automated Agents)
Automated agents should flag (not act unilaterally on) reservations or interactions showing:
- Repeated same-day, multiple-room bookings under different guest names using the same payment card.
- A booking name that does not match the payment cardholder with no reasonable explanation (e.g., known corporate travel arranger).
- Requests to change a confirmed reservation's payment method to a new, unverified card shortly before arrival.
- Any request pushing for cash refunds to a different party than the original guest of record.

## 6. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- Automated agents must never authorize an eviction, must never threaten legal or law-enforcement action, and must never confirm or deny an active law-enforcement investigation.
- Any report of harassment, threats, or safety concerns from a guest must be escalated immediately to a human agent/security, in addition to being logged verbatim (without embellishment) in the incident system.
