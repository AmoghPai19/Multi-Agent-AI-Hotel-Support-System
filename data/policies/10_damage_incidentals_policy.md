# Damage & Incidentals Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-DMG-010 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 1.2
**Owner:** Housekeeping / Finance

## 1. Purpose
Defines how property damage, missing items, and excessive cleaning are assessed and charged after a guest's stay.

## 2. What Counts as Chargeable Damage
- Burns, stains, or tears to furniture, linens, or carpets beyond normal use.
- Broken fixtures, electronics, or décor items.
- Missing items (towels, remote controls, artwork, etc.) not returned at check-out.
- Excessive cleaning required beyond standard turnover (e.g., biohazard cleanup, heavy odor removal, pet stains in non-pet-designated rooms).
- Smoking in a designated non-smoking room (flat remediation fee, disclosed at booking — see POL-SMOKE-018).

## 3. What Does NOT Count as Chargeable Damage
- Normal wear and tear from ordinary use.
- Pre-existing damage documented by staff at check-in or reported promptly by the guest upon discovery.
- Minor spills that standard housekeeping resolves within routine turnover time.

## 4. Assessment & Charging Process
1. Housekeeping/maintenance documents the issue with photos and a written description at the time of discovery, ideally before the next guest checks into that room.
2. A cost estimate is prepared using the property's standard replacement/repair cost schedule (not arbitrary amounts).
3. The guest is notified of the specific charge, the reason, and supporting documentation (photo + description) before or immediately after the charge posts to the card used for the incidental hold.
4. Charges above a defined threshold (e.g., $300) require manager approval before posting.

## 5. Guest Dispute Rights
A guest may dispute a damage charge by providing their own evidence (e.g., photos of the room's condition at departure) or by requesting the property's documentation. Disputes are handled per POL-DISP-020 if not resolved directly with the property.

## 6. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- Automated agents must never post or confirm a damage charge without linked photo/written documentation on file.
- Automated agents must never estimate or invent a damage cost — only relay figures from the approved cost schedule or a human-confirmed amount.
- Any damage charge dispute must be escalated with the guest's counter-evidence attached, not resolved unilaterally by waiving or upholding the charge.
