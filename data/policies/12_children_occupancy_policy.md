# Children, Occupancy & Extra Bed Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-OCC-012 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 1.2
**Owner:** Front Office / Revenue Management

## 1. Maximum Occupancy
- Maximum occupancy per room is set by local fire code and posted in-room and at booking; automated agents must never confirm a booking that exceeds the posted maximum for the selected room type.
- Standard maximums are typically: 2 adults + 1-2 children under a specified age (commonly 12 or 17) per standard room, with suites/connecting rooms allowing more.

## 2. Children Stay Free Policy
- Children under a specified age (commonly 12, 17, or 18 depending on brand/property) stay free when sharing existing bedding with a paying adult, with no separate charge, subject to the room's maximum occupancy.
- An additional charge applies for a rollaway bed, crib, or additional bedding request, disclosed at time of request (commonly $15-$35/night, cribs often free).

## 3. Unaccompanied Minors
- Guests under 18 (or the property's stated minimum, commonly 18-21) may not book or check into a room as the sole registered guest.
- Any indication that a minor is checking in alone, or that a room is booked by an adult but will be occupied only by minors without adult supervision, must be escalated to a manager before check-in is completed.

## 4. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- Automated agents must check the room type's maximum occupancy before confirming any booking that lists children or additional occupants, and must clearly state any extra-bed/crib fee before the guest confirms.
- Automated agents must never process a check-in or reservation where the primary/registered guest is confirmed or strongly indicated to be a minor.
