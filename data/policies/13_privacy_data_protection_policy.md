# Privacy & Data Protection Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-PRIV-013 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 2.0
**Owner:** Legal / Data Protection Officer

## 1. Purpose
Governs collection, use, storage, and disclosure of guest personal data, in line with applicable law (e.g., GDPR for EU/UK guests, CCPA/CPRA for California residents, and equivalent state/national lodging and privacy regulations).

## 2. Data Collected
Name, contact details, payment information (tokenized/PCI-scoped, not stored in plaintext — see POL-PAY-006), government ID details (verified but not necessarily retained long-term, per local law), stay history, loyalty program activity, and preferences (e.g., accessibility needs, room preferences).

## 3. Purpose Limitation
Guest data is used only for: fulfilling the reservation and stay, legal/tax compliance, fraud prevention, loyalty program administration, and — only with explicit opt-in consent — marketing communications. Data must not be used for purposes the guest was not informed of at collection.

## 4. Guest Rights
Depending on jurisdiction, guests may have the right to: access their data, request correction, request deletion ("right to be forgotten," subject to legal retention requirements like tax records), data portability, and opt out of sale/sharing of personal information (notably under CCPA/CPRA) or of marketing (under GDPR/CAN-SPAM/CASL).
Any such request received by an automated agent must be logged and routed to the Data Protection Officer / Privacy team within the jurisdiction's required response window (commonly 30 days under GDPR, 45 days under CCPA), not resolved by the agent itself.

## 5. Data Sharing with Third Parties
- OTAs, payment processors, and loyalty partners receive only the data necessary for their specific function, under a data processing agreement.
- Guest data is never sold to unrelated third parties without explicit consent where required by law.
- Law enforcement requests for guest data must be routed to Legal/management with valid legal process (subpoena/warrant where required) — front-line and automated agents must never independently disclose guest records to a party claiming to be law enforcement.

## 6. Retention & Security
- Personal data is retained only as long as necessary for the stated purpose or legal requirement (commonly 3-7 years for financial/tax records, shorter for marketing consent unless renewed).
- All systems handling guest PII must use encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access control, and audit logging of access.

## 7. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- Automated agents must never disclose one guest's reservation, folio, or personal details to another party without verifying that party's identity/authorization against the reservation.
- Any data subject request (access, deletion, opt-out) must be acknowledged to the guest and escalated to the privacy team — an automated agent must not attempt to fulfill a deletion request unilaterally, since legal retention obligations may override it.
- Never log full payment card numbers, government ID numbers, or passwords in plaintext conversation transcripts or application logs.
