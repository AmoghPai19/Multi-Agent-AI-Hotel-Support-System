# Accessibility Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-ADA-014 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 1.2
**Owner:** Guest Relations / Facilities

## 1. Purpose
Ensures compliance with disability access laws (e.g., ADA Title III and the 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design in the US, equivalent accessibility legislation elsewhere) and consistent, dignified guest experience for guests with disabilities.

## 2. Accessible Rooms
- A defined minimum percentage of rooms (per local building code, commonly ~4-5% of total rooms in newer US construction) must meet mobility-accessibility standards (roll-in showers or accessible tubs, grab bars, lowered fixtures, wider doorways) and a smaller subset must meet communication-accessibility standards (visual alarms, doorbell/phone signalers).
- Accessible rooms must be held for guests who request them and released to general inventory only per the property's accessible-room release policy (commonly held until a defined cutoff, e.g., 48 hours before arrival, if not booked as accessible-specific).
- Accessible rooms should never be offered as an "upgrade" swap that removes them from availability for guests who specifically need them, without an equivalent accessible alternative.

## 3. Service Animals
See POL-PET-011 §1 — service animals are always permitted at no charge, independent of this policy's room provisions.

## 4. Communication Accommodations
- TTY/relay service availability, or equivalent modern alternatives (SMS/chat-based communication) should be offered for guests who are deaf or hard of hearing.
- Materials such as menus and key information should be available in accessible formats (large print, braille where feasible, or read aloud by staff) upon request.

## 5. Requests & Reasonable Accommodation
- Reasonable accommodation requests (e.g., a refrigerator for medication, a specific room location near an elevator) should be fulfilled wherever operationally feasible and must not carry an extra charge solely because the request relates to a disability.
- A request that cannot be fulfilled must be explained honestly, with an offer of the closest available alternative.

## 6. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- Automated agents must never ask for proof or documentation of a disability to grant an accessible room or reasonable accommodation.
- Automated agents must flag any accessible-room request to ensure it is fulfilled from accessible inventory, not general inventory, and must not present an accessible room as optional/upgrade-only.
- Complaints about denied accessibility accommodations should be escalated immediately to a manager, given the legal exposure involved.
