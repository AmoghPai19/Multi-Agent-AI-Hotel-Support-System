# Loyalty Program Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-LOY-015 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 1.5
**Owner:** Loyalty / Marketing

## 1. Enrollment & Earning
- Enrollment is free and open to guests 18+ (or local age of majority).
- Points are typically earned per qualifying dollar spent on eligible charges (room + eligible F&B/spa), commonly 10 points per $1, excluding taxes, third-party OTA "merchant model" bookings (unless the OTA participates in a linked-earning agreement), and gift card purchases.
- Elite tier status is earned via qualifying nights or qualifying spend within a program year (typically Jan 1-Dec 31), with tiers such as Silver/Gold/Platinum/Diamond-equivalent unlocking escalating benefits.

## 2. Elite Tier Benefits (Illustrative Standard Tiers)
| Tier | Typical Qualifying Nights | Sample Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | 0 | Member rates, mobile check-in |
| Mid | 10-25 | Room upgrade (subject to availability), late check-out (2 PM) |
| Upper | 25-50 | Guaranteed late check-out (4 PM), lounge access, bonus points |
| Top | 50-75+ | Guaranteed room availability at 48-72 hrs, suite upgrade, walked-guest priority protection, dedicated service line |

Exact thresholds and benefits vary by brand program and current promotional terms; the agent should confirm current terms rather than assume fixed lifetime values.

## 3. Points Redemption & Expiration
- Points can typically be redeemed for free nights, upgrades, or partner transfers (airline miles, etc.) at posted redemption charts.
- Points expire after a defined period of account inactivity (commonly 12-24 months with no qualifying activity); reactivation offers may be available.
- Points are non-transferable to another person's account except via defined program mechanisms (e.g., pooling/sharing features), and have no cash value.

## 4. Status Matching & Challenges
Guests with elite status in a competing program may request a status match or challenge, evaluated case-by-case against documentation of their current status — this typically requires human/marketing team review, not automated approval.

## 5. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- Automated agents may confirm a member's current points balance and tier from account data, but must not promise discretionary benefits (like a specific upgrade) as guaranteed unless the program terms state it as guaranteed for that tier.
- Any point adjustment beyond routine stay-crediting (e.g., "my points are missing from a stay 3 months ago") should be escalated to loyalty support with the stay confirmation number for manual audit rather than adjusted directly by the agent beyond a small defined threshold.
