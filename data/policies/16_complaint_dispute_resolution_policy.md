# Complaint & Dispute Resolution Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-CDR-016 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 1.4
**Owner:** Guest Relations

## 1. Purpose
Defines a consistent, fair process for handling guest complaints and disputes that are not resolved by the specific policy that generated them (billing, refund, damage, etc.), and when to escalate to human staff, management, or external resolution.

## 2. Complaint Intake Standards
- Acknowledge every complaint promptly (automated agents: immediately; staff: within the shift or 24 hours for written/email complaints).
- Restate the issue back to the guest to confirm understanding before proposing a resolution.
- Never argue with or dismiss a guest's account of events; gather facts neutrally and check them against system records (folio, reservation log, cancellation log, etc.).

## 3. Service Recovery Framework (LEARN Model)
1. **Listen** — let the guest fully explain without interruption.
2. **Empathize** — acknowledge the impact on their stay/experience.
3. **Apologize** — a sincere apology is not an admission of legal liability.
4. **Resolve** — offer a resolution within the agent's authority (see thresholds below), or escalate.
5. **Notify** — log the incident and follow up if a promise (callback, credit, correction) was made.

## 4. Resolution Authority Thresholds
| Resolution Type | Automated Agent Authority | Requires Human/Manager |
|---|---|---|
| Apology + information | Yes | — |
| Refund/credit up to $150 equivalent, clearly policy-justified | Yes, with log entry | — |
| Refund/credit above $150, or any non-standard exception | No | Yes |
| Reversing a no-show/cancellation charge | No, unless clearly matches a documented exception (POL-CXL-002 §4) | Yes for anything ambiguous |
| Any legal threat, safety incident, discrimination complaint, or media/regulatory inquiry | No | Yes — escalate immediately to management/legal |

## 5. Formal/Escalated Disputes
If a guest is not satisfied after front-line/manager resolution, they may escalate to: corporate guest relations, the loyalty program's guarantee process (if applicable), their payment card issuer (chargeback — see POL-DISP-020), or, where applicable, an independent consumer/travel ombudsman or small-claims court. The property must not discourage a guest from pursuing any of these channels.

## 6. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- Automated agents must never make a legal admission of fault ("this is our fault and we are liable") — stick to factual acknowledgment and empathy language.
- Any complaint involving discrimination (based on race, religion, disability, national origin, sexual orientation, etc.), harassment, or safety must be escalated immediately and logged verbatim, in addition to any standard service recovery offered.
- Do not close out a complaint record until the promised action (refund, callback, correction) is confirmed complete.
