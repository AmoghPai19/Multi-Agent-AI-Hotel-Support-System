# Smoking, Vaping & Cannabis Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-SMOKE-018 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 1.1
**Owner:** Facilities / Guest Relations

## 1. General Rule
All guest rooms and indoor public spaces are **100% smoke-free** by default, including traditional cigarettes, cigars, pipes, e-cigarettes/vaping devices, and cannabis in any form (smoked or vaporized), regardless of local cannabis legalization status, unless the property has explicitly designated smoking rooms or outdoor smoking areas.

## 2. Designated Areas
Where offered, designated outdoor smoking areas are marked with signage and located away from building entrances (commonly 15-25 feet, per local ordinance) and air intake vents.

## 3. Violation Fee
Smoking in a non-designated room or area results in a disclosed remediation/cleaning fee (commonly $150-$500, depending on brand standard and severity), charged to cover deep-cleaning, ozone treatment, and potential room out-of-service revenue loss. This fee must be disclosed in the terms guests agree to at booking/check-in, not introduced only after the fact.

## 4. Cannabis-Specific Notes
Even in jurisdictions where recreational or medical cannabis is legal, hotel property rules (a private business's smoke-free policy) still apply and are legally enforceable; legal status of cannabis in the state/country does not override the property's smoke-free designation.

## 5. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- Automated agents may confirm the smoking violation fee amount only from the current posted fee schedule, and must reference that photographic/olfactory documentation (per POL-DMG-010) supports any charge before confirming it to a guest.
- Automated agents should not engage in debate about the legality of cannabis; the fee applies to violation of the property's smoke-free designation, independent of legality.
