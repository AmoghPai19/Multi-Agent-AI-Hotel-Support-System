# Security Deposit / Incidental Hold Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-SEC-019 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 1.2
**Owner:** Finance / Front Office

## 1. Purpose
Defines how incidental holds (sometimes called security deposits) are collected, held, and released for guest stays.

## 2. Standard Hold Amounts
- Typical incidental hold: a flat per-stay amount (e.g., $50-$100) or a per-night amount (e.g., $25-$50/night), taken as a **pre-authorization** on a credit/debit card at check-in, not an actual charge.
- Cash-paying guests are typically required to leave a cash deposit of a comparable amount, refunded at check-out after a room inspection, minus any incidentals/damage.
- Debit card holds may take longer to release than credit card holds (issuer-dependent) and guests should be informed of this distinction, since it affects their available balance rather than their credit limit.

## 3. Release Timing
- Holds are released automatically by the property's system at check-out (assuming no incidental charges), but the actual reappearance of funds is controlled by the guest's bank, typically **3-10 business days**.
- The property must never tell a guest an exact date funds will reappear beyond "your bank typically releases this within X business days" since the property does not control the issuing bank's timeline.

## 4. Converting a Hold into a Charge
If incidentals (minibar, damage, extra housekeeping, smoking fee, etc.) are incurred, the original hold must be **voided**, and a new, itemized charge for the actual amount owed must be processed — the property must not simply let an unrelated hold "convert" without an itemized record, to avoid guest confusion and disputed charges.

## 5. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- Automated agents must clearly disclose the incidental hold amount and that it is a hold (not a charge) before or at check-in confirmation.
- Automated agents must never guarantee a specific release date for hold funds; correct language is "typically released by your bank within 3-10 business days after check-out."
- Guest complaints about holds not releasing on time should be escalated with the checkout date and card type (debit vs. credit) if the delay exceeds the standard window, since this may need a manager's confirmation letter to the guest's bank.
