# Booking Disputes & Chargeback Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-DISP-020 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Version:** 1.3
**Owner:** Finance / Guest Relations

## 1. Purpose
Governs how the property handles guest-initiated payment disputes, both informal ("I was charged the wrong amount") and formal (a bank/card-network chargeback).

## 2. Informal Disputes (Direct to Property)
- The guest should always be encouraged to raise a billing concern directly with the property first, since this is faster to resolve than a formal chargeback and preserves the folio's audit trail.
- The agent pulls the itemized folio, compares it to the reservation's rate/terms and any service recovery credits already issued, and either: (a) confirms the charge is accurate and explains why, citing the specific rate/policy term, or (b) identifies an error and processes a correction/refund per POL-REFUND-003.

## 3. Formal Chargebacks (Card Network Dispute)
- Once a guest files a formal chargeback with their bank, the charge is provisionally reversed and the property must respond to the card network's inquiry within the network's deadline (commonly 7-20 days depending on network and reason code).
- The property's response package should include: signed registration card or e-confirmation showing the guest agreed to the rate/cancellation terms, folio, proof of stay (key usage, ID verification), and any service-recovery communication already provided.
- The property must not attempt to re-charge the guest's card for the disputed amount while a formal chargeback is in process; this must go through the card network's representment process instead.

## 4. Common Chargeback Reason Codes & Correct Handling
| Reason Code Category | Correct Property Response |
|---|---|
| "Service not as described" | Provide documentation of amenities/room delivered matching the booking description |
| "Duplicate charge" | Verify folio for actual duplicate; refund immediately if confirmed, contest with evidence if not |
| "Cancelled reservation, still charged" | Pull cancellation log; refund if cancellation was before cutoff, contest with the cancellation-policy acceptance record if after cutoff |
| "Fraud - I didn't make this booking" | Escalate to fraud/security review; do not simply refund without checking for a genuine unauthorized-use pattern versus friendly fraud |

## 5. Compliance Notes for Automated Agents
- Automated agents should always attempt informal resolution first and clearly explain how to formally dispute with their bank if unsatisfied — the property must never discourage a guest from exercising their chargeback rights.
- Automated agents must not fabricate or embellish documentation to contest a chargeback; only actual system records may be submitted.
- Any suspected fraud-related dispute must be escalated to the fraud/security team rather than resolved unilaterally by refund or denial.
